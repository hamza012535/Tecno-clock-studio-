## 4. Wallhaven & Lorem Picsum Wallpaper APIs
- **Wallhaven (`https://wallhaven.cc/api/v1/search`)**: Used via public SFW search API. Wallpapers are loaded directly from Wallhaven CDN URLs without re-hosting or proxying.
- **Lorem Picsum (`https://picsum.photos`)**: Used via public keyless API for random and dimension-matched lock screen wallpapers.
- **Ownership Notice:** **Tecno Clock Studio** uses the public APIs of Wallhaven and Lorem Picsum strictly for browsing and display, and **does not claim ownership of any images**. All wallpapers remain the property of their respective creators and owners.
